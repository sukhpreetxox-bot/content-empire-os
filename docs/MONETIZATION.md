# 💰 Monetization playbook (current as of 2026)

> Honest framing: AdSense/YPP approval is **YouTube's** decision and faceless AI
> channels are reviewed extra closely. No one can *guarantee* it. This plan
> maximises the odds and the speed.

## The thresholds (2026, two tiers)
| Tier | Subs | + one of | Gives |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Fan funding** | 500 | 3,000 watch-hrs / 12mo **OR 3M Shorts views / 90d** | tips, memberships |
| **2 — Full ad revenue** | 1,000 | 4,000 watch-hrs / 12mo **OR 10M Shorts views / 90d** | AdSense ad revenue |

Also required: account **≥ 30 days old**, **2-step verification**, **linked
AdSense**, **3 public uploads in 90 days**. Review takes **1–4 weeks** — apply
the moment you qualify.

## ⚡ Fastest path = Shorts
Shorts views (3M→Tier1, 10M→Tier2 in 90 days) accumulate far faster than
4,000 watch-hours. **So: high volume of strong Shorts + a few long-form for
watch-time and RPM.** Our pipeline already does both (`--format short`).

## 🚨 The real gate: the "inauthentic content" review (Jan 2026 crackdown)
YouTube does **not** penalise AI. It demonetises **mass-produced, templated,
repetitive, easily-replicable** content — "AI slop": *AI voice + stock footage +
no original insight + identical template every video.* In Jan 2026, 16 channels
(4.7B views) were removed.

**What survives:** a recognisable persona/brand, a real point of view, **format
variation across videos**, and transformative commentary.

## ✅ What we built so the channel is on the *safe* side
| Risk YouTube flags | Our countermeasure |
|---|---|
| No original insight | **Editorial gate** + Gemini prompt forced toward a *counterintuitive, specific* insight (rejects bare recaps) |
| Generic stock footage | **AI scene images** (Cloudflare FLUX), unique per video — no stock, no Content-ID |
| "Looks templated" | **Per-video layout variation** (`variant`: caption position/style, title position rotate) |
| No persona/brand | **Recurring brand**: intro emblem flash, persistent corner bug, branded outro CTA, consistent voice + navy/gold identity |
| Pure automation | **Human approval gate** (you approve every video on the dashboard) |
| Repetitive structure | **Series framework** (the 4 Capital types) gives variety with coherence |

## 🧭 The brand strategy (recurring = "real channel")
- **One persona**: Quiet Capital — calm, authoritative, philosophical. Same voice
  (Kokoro am_michael), same palette (navy + gold), same emblem on every video.
- **Recurring signatures**: intro flash, corner bug, outro "Build what cannot be
  taken. ▸ Subscribe". These repeat every video → recognisable.
- **Series**: every video belongs to one Capital type
  (Attentional / Psychological / Character / Sovereign) — like a TV show's segments.
- **Consistent cadence**: drip via `publish_schedule` (don't dump; look organic).

## 📋 Your action checklist
1. **Claim** @quietcapital on YouTube + Instagram now (before public).
2. Channel basics: avatar = `assets/brand/quiet-capital-avatar.png`, banner +
